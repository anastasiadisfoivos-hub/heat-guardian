# R8 — ISEF / EUCYS rules and Greek pathways (researched 13 Nov 2026)

## Verdict
- **EUCYS is the real route.** Greece runs a national selection every spring; EUCYS 2027 is in **Genoa, Italy**.
- **ISEF 2027 is effectively closed to a student in Greece.** The Society's Find-a-Fair directory (2026–27) returns **"No fairs match"** for Greece (and Cyprus). There is no direct entry.
- **Self-wear test on 16–18 Nov:** if it is *passive* (normal day, no deliberate heating, only he wears it), ISEF classes it as **IRB-exempt**. Still document it *before* starting, so nobody has to take his word for it later.

## 1. ISEF human-participant rules (2027 International Rules)
- **The researcher as subject counts as human-participant research** ("studies in which the researcher is the subject"). Normally this needs IRB approval *before* any data collection. The rules never grant retroactive approval: approval must come "before experimentation begins" ([Rules for All Projects](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/international-rules/rules-for-all-projects/), Documentation 4 and 7).
- **The key exemption is student-designed invention or prototype testing.** It applies when the student is the *only* tester and "the testing does not pose a health or safety hazard". **IRB pre-approval is needed** if anyone else tests it, or if he introduces "a human variable… (e.g., amount of sleep, strength or endurance of tester)" ([Human Participants](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/international-rules/human-participants/)). Heating himself (sauna, exercise in heat, hot bath) to provoke heat stress would be that kind of human variable, and a hazard too, so it needs an IRB.
- **Secondary data is exempt.** Pre-existing, publicly available or published datasets with no interaction with people need no IRB. Data that is de-identified but *not* public needs a written de-identification certificate from the professional who provides it. CC-BY physiological datasets fit the exempt case; cite them and keep the licence.
- **Forms:** 1 (Adult Sponsor checklist), 1A (Student checklist + research plan), 1B (Approval), 3 (Risk Assessment, required for any human testing), 4 (Human Participants, IRB signs it) or the IRB letter from an institution. 2A is new for 2027 and required for every project ([2027 forms PDF](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Forms/All.pdf)).
- **IRB:** at least 3 members: an educator (not the Adult Sponsor), a school administrator, and a medical or mental-health professional.
- **Timing:** no more than 12 continuous months of research. For ISEF 2027 the work must fall between **January 2026 and May 2027**. Continuation projects need Form 7 and genuinely new work; repeating with more subjects doesn't count.
- **AI use** must be cited (AI Use Table, 2027 rules). Students must write the research plan, abstract and poster text themselves ([rules index](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/international-rules/)).
- **Eligibility:** grades 9–12, under 20 on 1 May before ISEF. Teams of up to 3.

## 2. Reaching ISEF from Greece
- **ISEF 2027:** Los Angeles, May 2027 ([ISEF](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef)). Entry is **only through an affiliated fair** covering the student's territory ([FAQ](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/faq/)).
- **No Greek fair:** [findafair.societyforscience.org](https://findafair.societyforscience.org/) lists none for Greece in 2026–27. The directory says it updates as fairs affiliate. The old *STEM Stars Greece* contest (US Embassy + SciCo) sent winners to ISEF in 2020, but I found no current edition.
- **Neighbouring fairs serve their own territories,** e.g. Italy's *I Giovani e le Scienze* (Milan, 13–15 Mar 2027, entry by 15 Jan 2027) and Germany's *Jugend forscht*.
- **Action:** email **[email removed] / [email removed]** to ask whether any affiliated fair accepts a student living in Greece. A new fair needs 1 year of ISEF-rules experience, so a Greek one is realistically for 2028 at the earliest.
- **2028 window (inference):** if the window pattern holds ("not before January of the year before"), data from Nov 2026 would fall outside it. ISEF 2028 would then need a Form 7 continuation with new 2027 work.

## 3. EUCYS — Greek national selection
- **Name:** Εθνικός Διαγωνισμός Νέων Επιστημόνων (National Young Scientists Contest).
- **2026 organiser:** Ministry of Social Cohesion & Family, General Secretariat for Demographic & Housing Policy, Youth Directorate (Dept D). Contact: **[email removed]** ([2026 call PDF](https://aerospace.uoa.gr/bcmAdmin/uploads/2026_639059591370592038-pdf.pdf)).
- **2026 cycle:** the call came out on 28 Jan 2026, with a deadline of **21 Apr 2026**.
  - Entrants must be aged 14–20, and university students only in their first year at most.
  - Projects can be individual or a team of up to 3.
  - Submit a Greek essay of up to 10 pages + 10 pages of figures + a 1-page innovation summary, signed via gov.gr (a parent signs for under-18s).
  - The committee picks 10 projects, then holds a video presentation and Q&A. The national team is up to 6 students.
  - The EU pays travel and accommodation for EUCYS; presentations there are in English.
- **2027 call:** not yet published. Expect the same pattern (call ~late Jan, deadline ~April 2027).
- **EUCYS 2027:** Genoa, Italy ([EC news, 14 Sep 2026](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/news/all-research-and-innovation-news/europes-young-research-talent-takes-cancer-climate-and-more-2026-09-14_en)). Dates are not announced yet; 2026 was in late September (Kiel, 22–27 Sep). Age range 14–20.

## 4. Before the 16–18 Nov self-wear test (do by 15 Nov, dated and signed)
1. **Written protocol (1–2 pages), dated before day 1.**
   - Aim; only he is the participant.
   - Passive normal-day wear: **no deliberate heat exposure, sauna or heavy exercise in heat**.
   - Oral thermometer schedule (e.g. not within 15 min of eating or drinking); what is logged.
   - Stop rules: feeling unwell, temp ≥ 38 °C, skin irritation or burn from the band, battery heat.
2. **Risk assessment (ISEF Form 3 content).** Cover battery and charging, skin contact and heat, hygiene of the oral thermometer. Get it signed by a **science teacher as Adult Sponsor**, plus a parent.
3. **Exemption determination in writing.** A short note signed by the teacher (ideally also a doctor) saying "student-only, no hazard, no human variable → IRB-exempt invention testing per ISEF Human Participants rules".
4. **Optional, cheap insurance:** convene a 3-person ad-hoc IRB (teacher, school administrator, doctor/nurse) and sign Form 4 *before* 16 Nov. That way any later fair or expansion (heat challenge, other wearers, older adults) is already covered.
5. **Keep a bound or dated lab notebook.** Raw logs go unedited into git with timestamps; record anything that deviates from the protocol. ISEF 2027 requires the notebook on check-in and judging days.
6. **Before testing on anyone else** (e.g. older adults, the WAICY partner): full IRB, informed consent, and parental consent for minors, all first. Give no medical advice to participants.
